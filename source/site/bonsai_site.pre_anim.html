How it works

Three minutes to set up. One number every day.

STEP 1

Connect your accounts

Link checking, cards and loans once. Sprout reads them, never moves money, and keeps everything up to date on its own.

STEP 2

Sprout finds your plan

Your paycheck, the bills that repeat, your debts and pay-later plans, all found from your history. Untick anything that looks wrong.

STEP 3

Spend without the guesswork

Every morning you get one number: what you can spend today, after bills and debt are covered. Spend less, and tomorrow gets more.
