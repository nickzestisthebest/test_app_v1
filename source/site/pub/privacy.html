Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

The short version

  • We never sell your personal information or share it for advertising.
  • Your budget and plan are stored on your phone. If you use bank sync, an encrypted backup is kept on our server so a new phone gets your plan back. You can turn it off.
  • Bank connections are read-only and go through Plaid. Sprout never sees your bank password and can never move money.
  • Most questions you ask are answered on your phone. Only some go to our AI provider, with a short summary instead of your full history.

What we collect

  • Account details you enter: name, email, phone, date of birth and ZIP code.
  • Money details you enter or connect: income, bills, debts, goals and transactions.
  • Bank data through Plaid, if you connect a bank: account names, the last four digits, balances and transactions, and for cards and loans, interest rates and minimum payments.
  • Questions you ask Ask Sprout, and screenshots or screen recordings you choose to scan. From a recording, Sprout picks a few still images on your phone and sends only those.
  • Cancel for me requests: the service, the name and email on that account, and the authorization you gave, with its date and time.
  • Credit reports you choose to add. Before anything leaves your phone, Sprout removes your Social Security number, birth date, address, phone number and full account numbers. The rest is read once by our AI provider to pull out accounts, balances, payment history and inquiries. The results are stored on your phone (and in your encrypted backup, if it's on). We don't keep the report file.
  • Phone notifications, if you turn them on: your phone's notification address and a short, encrypted list of upcoming reminders, so our server can send them at the right time. Apple or Google deliver them. Turning notifications off deletes both.
  • Usage counts, such as how many AI answers or scans you used, to apply plan limits. We don't use advertising trackers.

Where it's stored

Your budget, plan, history and profile are stored on your device. Our servers, hosted by Vercel with storage by Upstash, keep only what the app can't do on the phone: encrypted bank connection tokens, usage counts, Cancel for me requests, and, if backup is on, an encrypted backup of your plan. The name and email in Cancel for me requests are encrypted.

Backup

When you use bank sync, Sprout keeps an encrypted copy of your plan, transaction history and streak on our server, so signing in on a new phone or after your phone's storage is cleared brings it back. Only your access code together with your Sprout account can restore it. Turn it off in Settings, under Bank sync and AI, and the copy is deleted right away. Deleting your account deletes it too.

Bank connections and Plaid

Sprout uses Plaid Inc. to connect to your bank. You sign in on Plaid's page, and your bank login is never shared with Sprout. Plaid's handling of your information is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. Connections are read-only. You can remove a bank any time in Settings, under Connected accounts, and its connection is deleted from our server.

AI answers and scans

When Ask Sprout needs AI to answer, your question and a short summary of your spending are sent to Anthropic, which writes the answer. When you scan screenshots or a screen recording, the images are sent to Anthropic to read the transactions and are not kept by Sprout. Under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, it may not train its models on this content. Questions answered on your phone never leave it.

How we use your information

To run Sprout and show your plan, keep your account secure, apply plan limits, carry out Cancel for me requests you approve, and answer you when you contact us.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that help run Sprout, under contract and only for that purpose: Plaid (bank connections), Anthropic (AI answers), Vercel (hosting) and Upstash (storage). When paid plans launch, a payment processor will be added here.
  • A company you ask us to contact through Cancel for me, which receives only what's needed: your name, the email on that account and your request.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information or share it with other companies for their marketing.

Your choices

  • See, export, correct or delete your data in Settings.
  • Disconnect a bank, or withdraw a Cancel for me request before we start on it.
  • Ask us to delete what our servers hold by emailing [your support email].

California and other state residents have additional privacy rights, and we honor them for everyone.

Face ID and voice

Face ID is handled by your phone. Sprout keeps only an ID for the passkey on your phone, and your face data never leaves your device. When you talk to log a purchase, your phone's own speech recognition turns it into text; Apple or Google handle that speech under their own privacy policies.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Bank connection tokens and Cancel for me contact details are encrypted at rest. Sprout never asks for your passwords to other services.

How long we keep it

We keep server data while your account is active. A bank connection is deleted when you disconnect it, and your backup is deleted when you turn backup off or delete your account. We keep a record of each Cancel for me authorization as proof of your permission.

Children

Sprout is for adults 18 and older in the United States.

Accuracy

Balances and transactions come from your bank through Plaid and can be delayed or incomplete. Sprout's categories, totals and estimates are calculated from that data and can be wrong. Check your bank's records before relying on them. See section 4 of the Terms.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and tell you in the app.
