Other ways to close a gap
  • Ask your boss if you can get paid early or get part of the bonus early.
  • Call your card companies about a hardship plan before you miss a payment.
  • Sell something you don't use, or pick up a few extra shifts.
  • Ask family to float a small amount until the bonus.
  • If you're overdrawn, fix it fast so fees don't pile up.
  • Avoid payday loans, cash advances and new Klarna or Affirm plans.

How Sprout worksThe plan in plain words
  • Your checking account is a bucket. Payday pours money in. Bills and spending pour money out.
  • Some payments, like buy-now-pay-later plans or a loan you marked as “can wait”, get paused when the bucket is low and paid as soon as there’s room.
  • Each day you get a spend limit so the bucket doesn't run dry before payday. Money you don't spend is spread over the rest of the pay period.
  • Any money left after a paycheck goes to paused payments first, then your savings goals, then the debt with the highest interest.

Before you let any payment wait, check whether that company charges a late fee.
